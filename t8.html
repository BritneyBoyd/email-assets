Welcome to the Superhost Masterclass. We're Brit and Michael, and we're genuinely glad you're here.

Everything in this course comes from our own properties: Ananda Eco House, the run-down Montville place we brought back to life in 2021, and Linden Rose Barn after it. Every checklist and template inside is something we actually use, and the course unlocks exclusive discount codes for the platforms and tools hosts rely on: Airbnb, Booking.com, Hostaway, Lodgify, Minut and more.

Let us tell you where all those checklists really came from. The night before our very first booking at Ananda went live, we were still unpacking linen and wrestling doonas into covers. And we very nearly missed something small: the bathroom door only locked from the outside. No lock on the inside at all. Imagine being a guest in a stranger's house and discovering that. It's the kind of detail you only catch when you stop thinking like an owner and start thinking like the person staying.

So before you open a single module, do one thing. Walk through your space (or the space you're dreaming about) as if you were a guest arriving at 9pm in the rain, suitcase in hand. Try every door. Whatever feels missing in that moment, write it down. That list is your starting point, and the course will meet you exactly there.

Start with Module 1 when you're ready. There's no pace to keep. Do it right, not fast.
